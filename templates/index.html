Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prepare for a fasting blood test?

For tests like Fasting Blood Sugar or Lipid Profiles, you should avoid eating or drinking anything (except water) for 10-12 hours before your scheduled collection time.

When will I receive my diagnostic report?

Most routine tests (like CBC, Thyroid, Liver panels) are processed and delivered securely to your email within 24 hours of sample collection.

How do I pay for my test?

You do not need to pay online. To ensure trust, payment is collected directly by our verified rider via Cash or UPI at the exact moment your sample is collected.
