The IHC Diagnostics Mission

Who We Are

Backed by the trusted expertise of IHC Diagnostics, CareDrop is Yamunanagar's premier healthcare aggregator. We partner strictly with highly-rated and NABL-accredited laboratories to ensure absolute accuracy and hygiene for every patient we serve.

Our Motive

We built CareDrop to eliminate the friction in modern healthcare. You shouldn't have to wait in crowded clinic lines or negotiate prices. Our platform provides absolute transparency, allowing you to compare lab costs, book certified phlebotomists to your doorstep, and receive your digital reports seamlessly.
